//!

//!
//! - Domain: `[−∞, +∞] × [−∞, +∞]`
//! - `hypot(±0, ±0)` is +0
//! - `hypot(±∞, qNaN)` is +∞
//! - `hypot(qNaN, ±∞)` is +∞.
//! - May raise overflow or underflow
